Case Study

Clario

A minimal productivity dashboard for creatives — designed to organize tasks, content, and brand assets in one clean, distraction-free workspace.

RoleProduct & UX Designer
Timeline6 weeks
ClientClario (concept)
PlatformWeb & iOS
ToolsFigma, Framer
Overview Brand Overview

Minimal productivity dashboard for creatives to organize tasks, content, and brand assets in one clean, distraction-free workspace. Built for speed, clarity, and creative flow.

Pain Problem Statement

Solopreneurs juggle multiple roles — marketing, client management, product development, and content creation — without a team to delegate to. Traditional project management tools (Trello, Asana, Notion) are built for teams and often feel bloated, requiring extra setup, manual updates, and discipline that solo operators rarely have time for.

As a result
  • Tasks fall through the cracks because updates aren’t automated.
  • Context switching between tools drains focus.
  • Solopreneurs spend more time managing their workflow than actually executing.

Clarity in Every Click.

Designed for people who move fast, think sharp, and expect better from their digital tools.

Solution How Clario Fits

For Clario, the challenge was clear: create a productivity dashboard that feels effortless, intuitive, and self-managing, so solo founders can focus on creation, not coordination.

Key pain points we aimed to solve
  • Excessive setup friction — users abandon tools that take too long to configure.
  • Manual task management — constant updates, moving cards, reminders.
  • Fragmented workflows — switching between tools, scattered notes & assets.
  • Decision fatigue — what project do I tackle first?
01

Strategy & Approach

We positioned Clario as the anti-bloat tool for solopreneurs. While traditional project management apps serve teams, Clario focuses on the individual — simplifying, decluttering, and automating. The approach centered on three principles:

  • Clarity — remove setup friction and make the system usable in minutes.
  • Control — give solopreneurs a dashboard that feels like “home base” for their day.
  • Confidence — leverage AI to reduce overwhelm and guide decisions.
02

UI/UX Visual Workflow

The visual workflow was designed to feel effortless and distraction-free:

  • Minimalist dashboard with clear hierarchy.
  • Quick-add tasks and drag-drop boards.
  • Integrated notes, content, and assets.
  • Progressive onboarding that feels natural.
03

AI Integration

The AI layer brings intelligence and removes manual effort:

  • Smart prioritization — surfaces what matters.
  • Auto-organization — tags & sorts tasks automatically.
  • Adaptive suggestions — learns the user’s style.
  • Effortless updates — talk to it instead of managing it.
JM

“I stopped losing tasks in Trello — Clario just knows where everything should live.”

Jason Mitchell

Marketing Strategist

Outcome Projected Brand Impact
  • Faster time to value — users can set up their first project in under 2 minutes.
  • Reduced friction — AI suggestions cut down manual organization by ~60%.
  • Higher engagement — early beta users report returning daily, not weekly.
  • Stronger brand intuitiveness — the visual voice reinforces the promise: calm + clever.
Reflection What I’d take forward

The biggest lesson from Clario was that restraint is a feature, not a compromise. Every time we were tempted to add a setting or a step, we asked whether a solo operator would actually use it on day one — most of the time, the answer was no. Designing for one person instead of a team changed almost every decision, from information density to how AI suggestions were surfaced without feeling intrusive.
