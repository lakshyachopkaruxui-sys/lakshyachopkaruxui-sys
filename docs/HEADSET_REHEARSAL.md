# Quest 3S rehearsal

## Start

1. Open the latest hosted HTTPS link directly in **Quest Browser on the headset**.
   If the private site asks you to sign in, use the account that owns the site.
2. Enable hand tracking and put down the controllers. Choose **Enter headset**.
   Accept the requested XR/hand permissions.
3. In **The World of Stillness**, confirm you see the actual room/classroom with a digital seam and hand cues.
   A furnished synthetic room means you are in desktop preview, not this route.

## Complete the journey

4. Pinch both sides of the seam with thumb and index finger. Pull apart with
   relaxed elbows. Hold a fully open tear steady for about 1.2 seconds.
   You do not need to physically walk forward to cross.
5. In **The World of Golden Growth**, check the forest in each eye: no room pixels should show through. Look up for
   the sun, clouds and birds; listen for the forest ambience.
6. Open the forest tear. Check that the water stream, foam and droplets remain
   below your hands and meet the forest floor. Cross into **The World of Drowned Dreams**, the underwater world.
   No real-room pixels should show through in either eye. Look up for the water
   surface, whale, sharks and rays; inspect fish, coral and shells around the
   sandy route. Listen for the quieter, muffled water and distant calls.
7. Repeat into **The World of the Watching Shadow**, the violet dimension. Check the denser upward stars, white jellyfish and clear ground. After
   settling, find the distant pale eyes, look away, then back: the shadow should
   slowly close the distance off-screen and freeze when seen. It must remain
   well away from you. Listen for the restrained eerie layers. Open another tear and confirm
   real-room passthrough appears inside it, then across the view after crossing.

Turn to a different direction before placing a tear and repeat a crossing. The
arrival should face the opening, keep the floor level and leave your tracked
position unchanged. Confirm chapter titles fit comfortably in both eyes.

## Recall help without breaking immersion

- Wait 20 seconds after entry: the idle hint should fade away.
- Face both open palms toward yourself, in view at relaxed chest height, and
  hold for 1.5 seconds. Field notes should appear once and stay in that location.
- Keep holding: they should not toggle repeatedly. Lower/turn your hands, then
  repeat the gesture to close them. Reopen and wait: they fade after 20 seconds.
- Pinching/tearing should never trigger this gesture. Starting a tear dismisses
  notes and restores the contextual tearing cue. Verify the gesture seated too.
- Check that enlarged italic chapter names and the upright serif instructions
  fit both eyes, and that the gesture does not invoke a Quest system action.

## Check interruptions once

8. While holding a partial tear, open the Quest system menu. Return, open your
   fingers, then pinch again. The app must not cross while the menu has focus.
   An interrupted partial tear closes; an already-started crossing pauses.
9. Lower your hands briefly out of tracking, then bring them back. Exit and
   re-enter once to check fresh seam placement and the real-room starting state.
10. Rehearse casting if people will watch from a laptop. Confirm framing, sound
   and delay on the actual network you will use for the presentation.

## What still needs this test

Software tests cannot establish passthrough clarity, stereoscopic correctness,
headset frame rate, comfort, physical tracking or live casting. If something is
wrong, capture the exact world/action and, if possible, a headset recording.
Do not describe the experience as device-verified until this rehearsal passes.
