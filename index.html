How I Work

A simple, repeatable process that keeps design decisions grounded in real user problems — not just good taste.

01 · Discover

Understand the problem

Stakeholder interviews, user research, and competitive teardown to find where the real friction lives.

02 · Define

Frame the right problem

Synthesize findings into a clear problem statement, personas, and success metrics everyone agrees on.

03 · Design

Explore, then commit

Wireframes to high-fidelity UI, stress-tested against edge cases and the design system.

04 · Deliver

Test, ship, iterate

Usability testing, developer handoff, and post-launch iteration based on real behavior.

About

Hi, I’m Lakshya.

I’m a UX/UI designer who likes taking complicated products and making them feel obvious. Most of my work sits at the intersection of research, interaction design, and visual systems — I care as much about the empty states as the hero screen.

Before going independent, I designed for early-stage startups across fintech, healthcare, and productivity tools, usually joining pre-product-market-fit and staying long enough to see the metrics move. I work closely with engineering, so what ships looks like what was designed.
