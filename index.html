Using Quest 3S or watching from a laptop?

Open this site in Quest Browser on your Quest 3S. Enable hand tracking, put down the controllers, and choose Enter headset. You begin in the real room around you.

Movement guidance fades after 20 seconds. On desktop, press H for field notes. In the headset, hold both open palms toward yourself for 1.5 seconds, with your elbows relaxed and your hands in view. Repeat the gesture to close the notes, or let them fade after 20 seconds. Tearing cues appear when you interact with the seam.

After crossing the tear, the forest, underwater sanctuary and violet landscape completely surround you. Tear through the violet dimension to return to your room. Desktop preview begins and ends in a walkable virtual room and needs a mouse and keyboard. Touchscreen phone controls are not supported.

To watch on a laptop, open Meta casting, then choose Cast → Web → Computer in the headset. Use the same Meta account and Wi-Fi.

A local development address beginning with localhost only works on that computer. For the headset, use the published HTTPS link or your computer’s HTTPS network address.
