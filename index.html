Using Quest 3S or watching from a laptop?

Open this site in Quest Browser on your Quest 3S. Enable hand tracking, put down the controllers, and choose Enter headset. You begin in the real room around you.

On desktop, press H for field notes, N to toggle narration, and V to replay it. In the headset, hold either index fingertip inside the Field notes circle until its ring fills. Move your finger away before repeating. While notes are open, the Voice and Replay circles control narration. Notes fade after 20 seconds; tearing hides them.

Cross into the ocean, then the shadow world, then the forest. The forest's next tear returns to your room. Desktop preview begins and ends in a walkable virtual room and needs a mouse and keyboard. Touchscreen phone controls are not supported.

To watch on a laptop, open Meta casting, then choose Cast → Web → Computer in the headset. Use the same Meta account and Wi-Fi.

A local development address beginning with localhost only works on that computer. For the headset, use the published HTTPS link or your computer’s HTTPS network address.
